# Further literature

The references attached to definitions, conventions, and constructors in the
manual are the primary sources for those sections. The shorter list below
points to further work that is especially relevant to two uses of the package.

## Mathematical physics

*Categorical Time-Reversal Symmetries* develops symmetry questions for tensor
categories motivated by topological phases [wen2026categorical](@cite).

## Arithmetic

*Fusion Categories over Non-Algebraically Closed Fields* develops the
arithmetic theory behind splitting and scalar extension
[sanford2025fusion](@cite).

*Tambara--Yamagami Categories over the Reals: The Nonsplit Case* studies a
concrete family where the defining field changes the category
[plavnik2025tambara](@cite).
